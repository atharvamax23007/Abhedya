System Guide

Zero-Internet Kote Management Protocol

Abhedya operates as a closed-loop tactical intranet for armory and inventory workflows without relying on external cloud connectivity.

  • Air-Gapped Authorization: Approvals are encoded into cryptographically signed tokens for offline verification.
  • Biometric & Token Checkout: Issuance pairs authorized request tokens with live operator verification.
  • Local Ledger Continuity: Endpoints maintain local transaction caches and synchronize when intra-base links are active.
